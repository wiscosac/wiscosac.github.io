## Question of the Week
  * **Question**: What are your predictions for the teams that will make the MLB Wild Card round? What stats/features could you use to compare these teams and rank them against one another?
  * **Important Stats/ Features**:
    * High-quality playoff teams usually have a strong bullpen
    * Need to consider opponent pitcher quality
    * Recent success (is the team hot?)
    * Playoff experience
    * Special conditions in the playoffs:
      * Added bullpen slot
      * Shorter rotation
      * Weather (cold in many ballparks)
